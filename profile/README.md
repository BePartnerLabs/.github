# BePartnerLabs

> **We don't outsource. We partner.**

BePartnerLabs is a boutique partnership lab for companies that compete on digital performance. We embed with your team in focused sprints to diagnose, redesign, and scale the metrics that actually drive revenue — conversion, retention, acquisition cost, and organic reach.

---

## What we do

We operate at the intersection of **growth strategy, digital architecture, and data**. Each engagement is a structured collaboration, not a project handoff.

| Layer | What we bring |
|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Audience segmentation, journey mapping, SEO baseline, KPI audit |
| **Architecture** | Information architecture, tool chain review (CRM, automation, analytics) |
| **Execution** | Sprint-based delivery with decision gates — no runaway scope |
| **Measurement** | GA4 dashboards, Looker Studio, 6-month KPI targets |

---

## How we work

**Sprint model** — not retainers, not permanent outsourcing.  
We scope, deliver, and hand off. Your team keeps the knowledge.

**Co-venture alignment** — we have skin in the game.  
Partnerships are structured so our upside is tied to your results.

**Decision gates** — no over-engineering upfront.  
Integrations like CRM automation or RAG pipelines are unlocked *after* baseline metrics are validated, not before.

## Our stack philosophy

We don't recommend tools because they're trendy. We recommend what's already in your organization and optimize from there.

Default choices are simple and boring on purpose:

- **Looker Studio** for dashboards (zero dev overhead)
- **GA4** for baseline measurement
- **N8N / Odoo** only at post-launch decision gates
